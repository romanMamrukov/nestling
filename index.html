Data & privacy

Data is stored locally in this browser. No server, account or cross-device synchronization is enabled. Clearing browser data may erase your records.

Nestling estimates recorded sleep patterns, not physiological sleep need. Follow your baby's cues and pediatric clinician's advice. Never delay feeding or care because of an app prediction. Practice evidence-based safe infant sleep.
